AI chip data hub, staging copy

A local rebuild of the AI Chip Sales and AI Chip Owners explorers from epoch.ai, reading the canonical tables in staging/ instead of Airtable. The models write those tables through pipeline/chip_schema.py; pipeline/build_staging.py merges them with the hand-maintained rows. See docs/schema.md for the schema and staging/README.md for how the folder is built.

AI Chip Sales

Chips shipped by Nvidia, Google, AMD, Amazon, Huawei and Cambricon, per quarter or cumulative, in units, H100-equivalents, power and cost.

AI Chip Owners

Who holds those chips: the hyperscalers, China, CoreWeave, xAI and everyone else, over time or at a single quarter.
