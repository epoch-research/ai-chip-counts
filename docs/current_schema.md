# The current schema: local exports vs Airtable

Sections 1 to 5 are generated by `pipeline/tools/schema_inventory.py`; rerun it after any export change. The findings section above them is written by hand and survives reruns. Airtable's side comes from the website's hourly mirror of each published view (`epoch-website-astro/src/public/data/generated/`), so it shows visible fields only. Cells give the literal column name; a dash means the table lacks that quantity.

## What's wrong, in brief

**One quantity, several names.** The median unit count is spelled three ways, and Airtable
disagrees with itself: the quarterly tables say `Number of Units`, sales cumulative says
`Number of units (median)`, owners cumulative says `Number of Units (median)`. The exporters
follow no rule either. Trainium always writes the bare form, so it mismatches every cumulative
table. AMD owners writes the `(median)` form into the one table that wants the bare form.

H100e is worse. Most tables pair `Compute estimate in H100e (median)` with tails called
`H100e (5th percentile)`. The cumulative tables spell the tails out in full. The sales
cumulative-by-chip table uses `H100e compute power` for all three.

`Total TDP (W)` is a median that doesn't say so, sitting beside a labelled 5th percentile.
The sales cumulative table does label it. The owners cumulative-by-designer table has no TDP
field at all, though every local file exports one.

**Columns that vanish.** Trainium exports `Power in MW` into two tables with no such field,
and `Source / Link` into two more. The import drops them without a warning.

**Chip specs in two places.** Airtable derives TDP, H100e and cost per row through lookups on
`chip_types`. The repo computes H100e, TDP and power from its own spec dicts. Nothing checks
that they agree.

**Airtable bookkeeping in the public data.** `Last Modified By`, `Last Modified`,
`Last Modified 2` and `Created` appear in the published CSVs.

**Names that depend on who wrote the row.** `Name` is the merge key, yet its format changes
by table and by family. In the quarterly sales table, Nvidia, TPU and Trainium write
`Q1 2024 - MI300X`, AMD writes a fiscal label into a calendar table (`FY24 Q1 - MI300X`), and
the hand-entered Huawei rows drop the dash. In sales cumulative by designer, Trainium borrows
the owners template. CoreWeave's owner rows omit the designer. Huawei's say `China (official)`
while their Owner column says `China`. One cumulative table puts the series start in the Name
and the other does not.

**Aliases.** The sales cumulative table says `H100` where everything else says `H100/H200`.
AMD chips are `MI300X` in the sales tables and `Instinct MI300X` in the owners tables.
Airtable has `Trainium1/Inferentia`, the exports `Trainium1`. The smuggling script writes
`China(smuggled)`, Airtable `China (smuggled)`. The column itself is `Chip manufacturer`,
while `chip_types` and the components base call it `Designer`.

**Dates.** Local files use `M/D/YYYY`, except the Nvidia fiscal file, which uses ISO, as
Airtable does.

**Coverage.** Four local files have no Airtable table: both fiscal-quarter files,
`nvidia_owners_quarters.csv` and `amd_owners_quarters.csv`. The CoreWeave files sit in their
own subfolder and reach Airtable by a separate hand step. Some rows exist only in Airtable:
Huawei and Cambricon in all six tables, xAI in the three owners tables, and smuggled China in
owners cumulative by designer.
